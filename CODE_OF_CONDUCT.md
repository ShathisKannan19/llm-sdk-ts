# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume positive intent.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or ban participants for violations.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to project maintainers privately.
For security reports, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
